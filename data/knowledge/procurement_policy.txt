Enterprise Procurement Policy
Version: 2026.1

1. Purchase orders with a value greater than CNY 100,000 are considered high-value purchases.

2. High-value purchases require additional procurement risk review when delivery delay exceeds 7 days.

3. Purchase orders exceeding CNY 500,000 require procurement manager review.

4. Suppliers with repeated delivery issues should receive increased monitoring.

5. Business actions that modify procurement records require authorized personnel approval.
