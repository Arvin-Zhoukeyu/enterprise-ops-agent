Supplier Risk Management Policy
Version: 2026.2

Supplier risk should be assessed using delivery performance,
payment incidents, historical risk events and supplier ratings.

A supplier should be considered elevated risk when two or more prior
distinct orders had serious delivery delays in the six calendar months
before the delivery being assessed. The window includes its starting date
and excludes the assessed order and incidents on or after its delivery date.
Only deliveries completed by the assessment date are observable evidence.

A delivery delay of 7 days or more is considered material.

A delivery delay of 15 days or more should be treated as a
high-severity delivery incident.

Suppliers with repeated material delays should be escalated for
manual procurement review.
